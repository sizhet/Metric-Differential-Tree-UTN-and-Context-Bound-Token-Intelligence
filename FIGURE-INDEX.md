# 27. Final Perspective

The figure pack is intentionally small.

Seven figures are sufficient to represent the full v1.0.0 argument without diluting the repository with redundant diagrams.

The visual logic is:

```text
SEE THE WHOLE
    ↓
BUILD IDENTITY
    ↓
LOCALIZE DECISIONS
    ↓
COMPOSE STRUCTURE
    ↓
ENCODE CONTEXT
    ↓
MEASURE ITS EFFECT
    ↓
REUSE INTELLIGENCE
```

The resulting visual thesis is:

> **Context is first folded into structural identity.
> Structural identity is then exposed through Context-Bound Tokens.
> Its effect is measured against a preserved baseline.
> Validated context finally becomes an address for reusable intelligence and future structural growth.**
